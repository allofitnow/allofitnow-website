Hover a name. It goes pure white; everything else falls away on a radial falloff measured from it. Two details are doing the work: the radius is an ellipse (the rows sit on 2.7 leading, so a circle would reach three rows up and only two names sideways — a vertical bar, not a glow), and each name carries a stable jitter on its own radius so the rim is ragged. Stable, because a per-hover reroll shimmers as you move along a row. Focus is also held while the cursor crosses the gaps between names — justified rows leave wide ones, and dropping focus in them strobes the whole section.
